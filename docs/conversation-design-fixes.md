From `docs/conversation-design.md` (SME team, red-teamed, advisor-reviewed, 2026-11-19). **Nothing here is implemented yet**; the operator picks fixes.

- **P0**: user-visible breakage, or wrong or unsafe answers. **P1**: clear UX gains. **P2**: polish.
- **Effort**: S / M / L.
